Timeline of Shapley-Based Explanation Methods
=============================================

.. container:: tl-intro

   Seventy years separate the Shapley value from today's explanations of tree ensembles, language
   models and foundation models. This timeline shows when the central concepts and methods of
   Shapley-based explainable AI were proposed, and which of them you can use in ``shapiq``.

   Every card is one method or concept, placed in the year of its publication and in one of eight
   lanes, from the game-theoretic foundations to software. Open a card for a short description,
   the reference, the classes that implement it in ``shapiq`` and a gallery example. Cards marked
   **In shapiq** are implemented; the others are important for the field but not part of the
   package. Filter by lane, search for a method or an author, or link to a card directly, e.g.
   ``timeline.html#kernelshap``.

   The selection follows the literature overview of the PhD thesis *Feature-Based Explainable AI:
   From Attribution to Interaction, From Static to Dynamic* (Maximilian Muschalik, LMU Munich,
   2026), extended by every method implemented in ``shapiq``. Its core contributions are marked
   with a star.
