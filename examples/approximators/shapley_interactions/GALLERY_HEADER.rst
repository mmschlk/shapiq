Shapley Interactions
--------------------

.. container:: shapiq-gallery-notes

   Estimators of Shapley interactions of any order. :class:`~shapiq.approximator.KernelSHAPIQ` and
   its inconsistent variant (SII, k-SII) and the faithful regressions
   :class:`~shapiq.approximator.RegressionFSII` and :class:`~shapiq.approximator.RegressionFBII`
   solve weighted regressions; :class:`~shapiq.approximator.SHAPIQ` and
   :class:`~shapiq.approximator.SVARMIQ` are Monte Carlo estimators for any index; permutation
   sampling estimates SII, k-SII, and STII.
