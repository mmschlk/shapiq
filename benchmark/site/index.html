How to read the scores ＋

nMSE. Squared estimation error divided by the sum of squared exact values. Zero is exact; lower is better. Games with zero exact-value energy are excluded.

Median and mean. The median describes typical error; the mean is more sensitive to outliers. Scores balance each family and setup, then instances, budgets, and runs. A median exactly between two values averages them.

Coverage. Successful runs out of planned runs. Missing or failed runs are never counted as zero error. Complete coverage appears first; partial scores use the available results. History uses complete coverage.

Time. Diagnostic runtime on matching hardware and thread settings. For cached games, measured runtime includes lookups rather than original model evaluations. Where recorded, estimated uncached time adds the saved evaluation costs, shared across each preparation batch. It is an estimate, not a fresh end-to-end measurement.
