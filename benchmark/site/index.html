About the numbers ＋

nMSE is squared coefficient error divided by ground-truth energy. Lower is better. Weights are equal across families, strata, games, budgets, and seeds. Median means the lower weighted median.

Incomplete panels remain unranked. Zero-energy games are excluded for every estimator. Paired ties use 1% relative error plus 1e-12. Ratings and history require a fixed, precomputed panel; ratings are specific to its competitor set.

Charts show each game separately, averaged across seeds. The default budget axis is B/d: coalition queries divided by the number of players. Colors, dashes, and markers identify estimators; hover or focus to highlight them. Top methods require complete results across all plotted budgets and are selected by mean nMSE. All methods includes estimators with at least one complete budget point; each point requires all seeds. Timings are diagnostic; resource checks alone do not qualify a timing leaderboard. The history is a retrospective comparison, not historical measurements.
