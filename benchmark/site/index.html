Performance at a glance

nMSE is squared coefficient error divided by truth energy; lower is better. Weights are equal across families, then strata, games, budgets, and seeds. Median is the lower weighted median. A cap selects the largest measured budget within it for each game; unavailable games remain missing. Incomplete panels are unranked.
